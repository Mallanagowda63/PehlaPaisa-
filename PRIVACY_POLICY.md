# PehlaPaisa — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 13 November 2026

PehlaPaisa ("the app") is a budgeting app. It helps you save first and then spend from simple "glasses". This policy explains what happens to your information. In short: **your data stays on your phone.**

## What the app stores

The app stores the following **only on your device**, in its private storage:

- the name you enter
- your monthly income and salary day
- your budget glasses, expenses and notes
- your goals, badges and settings

The app does **not** create an account, and it has **no servers**. None of this information is sent to us or to anyone else.

## What the app does not do

- No login and no cloud sync.
- No analytics of our own.
- No access to your bank accounts, SMS, contacts, camera or precise location.
- Your budget data (income, glasses, expenses, goals) is never sent anywhere, including to the ad provider.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from **Google AdMob** (a service of Google LLC) on some screens. Internet access is used **only** to load these ads; every budgeting feature works offline.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects from your device: the **advertising ID** (Android) or other device identifiers, **IP address** (from which an approximate location may be inferred), **device and app information** (model, OS version, app version, language), **ad interactions** (views and taps), and **diagnostic data**. Google collects and processes this under its own policies: [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Your choices:** on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's *Settings → Google → Ads* (or *Settings → Privacy → Ads*). Users in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

## Payments

PehlaPaisa **does not process payments** and never sees your bank details or UPI PIN. When you pay, it opens a UPI app installed on your phone (for example Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM). You scan the QR and complete the payment inside that app, under that app's own terms and privacy policy. PehlaPaisa only records the expense in your budget when you confirm that you paid.

## Notifications

Reminders (salary day, weekly check-in, daily reminder and low-glass alerts) are created and shown on your device. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.

## Sharing and export

The app shares something only when you choose to:
- **Share cards:** progress images. Amounts are hidden unless you turn them on.
- **Export data:** a JSON file of your data.

Both go through your phone's share menu, to apps you pick.

## Deleting your data

Use **Settings → Reset all data** in the app, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything, because there is no copy anywhere else. Phone backups of app data are turned off.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

## Financial information

The app shows general, educational money tips only. It does not give investment or financial advice.
