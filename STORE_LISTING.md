## 2. Texts (English)

**Short description** (Play, 80 characters max, 77 used):
> Savings app in Hindi & English: save first on salary day, then spend. Simple.

**Promotional text** (App Store, 170 characters max):
> Fill your Savings glass first on salary day, then spend from Rent, Food, Travel and Fun glasses. Private, works offline, made for India.

**Full description:**
> **Pehle bachao, phir kharcho — save first, then spend.**
>
> PehlaPaisa is a simple **budget planner and savings app** for India, in **Hindi and English**. It helps you build the "pay yourself first" habit, even on a small salary of ₹5,000–₹30,000 a month. Your money becomes glowing glasses on a shelf, so you always see what's left.
>
> Made for first jobs, students with a stipend, and anyone whose salary runs out before the month does. No complicated expense tracker, no SMS reading — just *paisa bachao* first, then spend calmly.
>
> 🪙 **Savings first.** On salary day, fill your Savings glass first. Then Rent, Food, Travel and Fun fill up with what's left.
> 🫙 **See what's left.** Every glass shows exactly how much you can still spend. Spending drains the glass. Savings is never used to cover other spending.
> 💸 **Pay with UPI.** Enter the amount in a glass, then pay in your own UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm…) by scanning the QR there. Come back, tap "Yes, paid", and the expense is added to that glass.
> 📅 **Weekly limits.** See how much you can spend each week so your glasses last the whole month.
> 🎯 **Goals.** Emergency fund, new phone, a trip: watch each goal glass fill up.
> 🔥 **Streaks and badges.** Keep your streak going and unlock badges like Pehla Paisa and Gullak Master.
> 📊 **Monthly report.** Where your money went, how much you saved, and the last 6 months at a glance.
> 🔔 **Gentle reminders.** Salary day, a Sunday check-in, and a warning when a glass runs low.
> 🇮🇳 **Made for India.** ₹ with Indian number format (₹1,00,000) and full Hindi support.
>
> **Private by design:** no login, no cloud, and no bank or SMS access. Your budget stays on your phone. PehlaPaisa never sees your bank details or UPI PIN. Free, supported by a few ads.
>
> General money tips are for learning only. This is not financial advice.

**Keywords** (App Store, 100 characters max, 97 used):

**Search words this listing targets (Play has no keyword field; it reads the title, short and full description):** budget app, savings app, budget app in Hindi, expense tracker, money manager, salary planner, paisa bachao, bachat. Use them naturally — Google penalises keyword stuffing.

## 4. Google Play — Data safety form

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **Yes** — only through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). The budget itself is never collected. |
| Is all data encrypted in transit? | **Yes** (the Ads SDK uses HTTPS) |
| Do you provide a way to request data deletion? | Yes: Settings → Reset all data, or uninstall. Ad data: users can reset their advertising ID in Android settings. |

Data types to declare for the Google Mobile Ads SDK (re-check Google's page "Prepare for Google Play's data disclosure requirements" for AdMob before submitting):

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Location → Approximate location (from IP) | Yes | Yes | Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention |
| App activity → App interactions | Yes | Yes | Advertising or marketing, Analytics |
| App info and performance → Crash logs, Diagnostics | Yes | Yes | Analytics, Fraud prevention |
| Device or other IDs | Yes | Yes | Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention |

Other Play Console declarations:
- **Ads:** **Yes, my app contains ads** (Google AdMob).
- **Advertising ID:** Yes — used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising and analytics (the SDK adds the `AD_ID` permission).
- **Target audience:** 18+ (a finance app). Not designed for children.
- **Content rating:** answer the questionnaire; expect "Everyone / 3+". There is no violence, gambling or user-generated content.
- **Financial features declaration:** the app is a **budgeting/expense tracker**. It does **not** offer loans, banking, investments or crypto. For payments, explain that it **opens the user's installed UPI app** and does not process payments itself.
- **Permissions:**
  - Notifications: reminders
  - Boot completed: restore reminders after a restart
  - Internet / network state: load ads only
  - Advertising ID (AD_ID): ads
- **App access:** no login is needed, so all features are open to reviewers.

## 5. Apple App Store — App Privacy ("nutrition label")

- **Data collection:** **Yes**, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK: Identifiers (Device ID), Usage Data (Product Interaction, Advertising Data), Diagnostics (Performance), Location (Coarse). Purpose: **Third-Party Advertising**, Analytics. Not linked to the user's identity.
- **Tracking:** No, as long as the app does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt (ads are then non-personalised on iPhone).
- The Google Mobile Ads SDK ships its own privacy manifest; `ios/Runner/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` covers the app's own code.
- **iOS ad units are not created yet:** add PehlaPaisa (iOS) in AdMob, create its 4 ad units, and paste the IDs into `lib/features/ads/ad_ids.dart` and `GADApplicationIdentifier` in `ios/Runner/Info.plist` (currently Google's test App ID, so iPhone builds show test ads).
- **Export compliance:** handled in Info.plist (`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO`).

**Notes for the App Review team** (paste into App Review Information):
> PehlaPaisa is an offline budgeting app; no login is needed. To try Pay with UPI: complete the short setup, tap a glass (for example Food), then Pay with UPI, and enter an amount. The app lists the user's installed UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm…) and opens the chosen one, where the user scans a QR and pays. On returning, the app asks whether the payment went through and records it. The payment itself happens entirely inside that app; PehlaPaisa never processes payments or sees bank details. If no UPI app is installed (as on a review device), the app shows "No UPI app found on this phone". All other features work offline without any account. The app shows Google AdMob ads on the Shelf, Goals, Report and Share screens.

**Age rating:** 4+. **Category:** Finance.

## 7. Release checklist

### Google Play (Android) — can be done from this Windows PC
1. Create a Play Console developer account (one-time $25).
2. Create the app, then fill in sections 1–4 above.
3. Upload `build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`. It is signed with the upload key in `android/key.properties`; **keep that file and `android/app/upload-keystore.jks` backed up**.
4. Start with **Internal testing**, then Closed testing (new personal accounts must run a closed test with testers before going to Production), then Production.

### Apple App Store (iOS) — needs a Mac
1. Join the Apple Developer Program ($99/year).
2. On a Mac with Xcode, open `ios/Runner.xcworkspace`. In **Signing & Capabilities**, choose your team. The bundle ID `com.pehlapaisa.app` is already set.
3. Run `flutter build ipa --release`, then upload with Xcode's Organizer or Transporter.
4. In App Store Connect, fill in sections 1, 2, 5 and 6, then submit for review.
5. **Before submitting, test on a real iPhone:** Pay with UPI opening Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm, and the reminder notifications. These could not be tested on Windows.

No Mac? The GitHub Actions workflow (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) compiles the iOS app on a cloud Mac on every push. Services like Codemagic can also sign and upload to the App Store without owning a Mac.
